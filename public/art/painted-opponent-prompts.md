# Painted opponent animation

The live renderer uses complete painted character cels (no bone rotation or sleeve stretching). Sources are transparent 4×2 atlases. `scripts/slice-painted-opponent.cjs` exports and registers 256px cels by the head; `renderScene.ts` keeps the actor seated and composites the table in front of the torso.
